01 / THE PROBLEM

Policies are easy to write.
They are harder to verify.

Natural-language instructions carry context that ordinary contracts cannot interpret reliably. CharterCore adds a consensus layer that turns those instructions into structured state before downstream logic relies on them.

02 / PROTOCOL

A semantic layer for contract policy.

01

Normalize

Validators independently extract actor, action, object, conditions, exceptions, scope and modality from a clause.

02

Compare

New clauses are evaluated against active charter state. Relationships are recorded instead of discarded after execution.

03

Resolve

Compatible clauses can activate. Conflicts can resolve through deterministic priority or remain explicitly blocked.
